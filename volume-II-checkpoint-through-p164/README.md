# Volume II checkpoint through printed page 164

This is a partial, cumulative checkpoint of *Complexe cotangent et
déformations II* (Springer LNM 283). It is not a complete Volume II release.

Included coverage is the seven-page front matter and printed pages 1–164 in
three aligned lanes:

- diplomatic French;
- corrected French;
- source-aligned English.

Each 171-page PDF was assembled without a new TeX build from the verified
through-p60 cumulative PDF followed by the retained one-page sealed artifacts
for printed pages 61–164. The p60→p61 join and the terminal p164 page were
rendered and visually checked in all three lanes.

The source snapshot contains exactly 513 TeX files: seven front-matter files
and 164 body files in each of the three lanes. It deliberately contains no
printed-p165 file.

Printed page 164 is the last valid boundary. Printed page 165 was reopened by
direct-authority review and is therefore excluded from every PDF, archive,
manifest, and coverage claim in this checkpoint. Further Volume II work
remains owned by its separate production task.
