# Volume I — complete working editions

This release seals the complete 380-page Volume I in two standalone forms:

- corrected French;
- source-aligned English.

Each edition has an independently buildable source package. The two editions
share stable semantic units and a machine-readable pre-Stacks scaffold; no
official Stacks tag is asserted or invented.

Deterministic build, structural, extraction, and visual validation is
`HARD_PASS`. Exact byte counts and SHA-256 identities are recorded in
`manifest.csv`, `validation.json`, and `VOLUME_I_FINAL_RECEIPT.json`.

This is the first complete-volume release in the continuing two-volume
lineage. Volume II will be added by its separate production task in a later
cumulative release.

No open licence is asserted. Repository and release access remain private
under the retained conservative rights disposition.
